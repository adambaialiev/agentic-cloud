# A daily report to Agentic Cloud you can see and turn off, and agent tools that keep themselves current

## What's new

- A daily report to Agentic Cloud. Once a day, at every start, and when the contact person changes, your Agentic Cloud sends us a short signed report: how many people, agents and sites you have, how much the agents worked in the last day, the release you run, and whether it is healthy. Counts only: no messages, code, files, mail, keys or names, except the contact person. It is signed by a key made inside your copy, so nobody else can send one in your name. If we cannot be reached, nothing changes for you.
- A contact person. The report names who we should write to about your Agentic Cloud. It starts as the first admin's name and email; an admin can change it at any time under Workplace › What we tell Agentic Cloud.
- What we tell Agentic Cloud. A new section on the Workplace screen, for admins, shows whether the report is on, who the contact is, when the last report went, and exactly what it said.
- The agent tools follow ours. Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode on your agents' machines now move to the versions we have tested, within a day of us moving, with no release needed. An admin who wants to stay on a version can still hold it, and then nothing changes until they let go.

## Fixed

- Nothing in this release.

## Needs you

- Check the contact person. Under Workplace › What we tell Agentic Cloud, make sure the name and email are the person we should write to about your Agentic Cloud, and change them if not.
- If you would rather not send the daily report, an admin can turn it off in the same section. One last report tells us it was turned off, and after that nothing more is sent.
