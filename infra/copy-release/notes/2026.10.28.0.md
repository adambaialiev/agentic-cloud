# A truer first sign-up, a greeting for newcomers, and the team view back

## What's new

- The first admin's sign-up page says plainly that the computers run in your company's own cloud account.
- Someone who joins an existing workplace is greeted as a newcomer, not as its founder.
- The team view is back: your teammates, whether they are here, and what their agents are doing.
- A proposal's pictures come first, framed at their own shape, and open faster.
- There is no Leaderboard. Your name in the account menu opens your own page instead.

## Fixed

- A proposal opened from a link opens at once, without waiting for the rest of the Workspace to load.
- On a secret's screen, each room you can share it with reads as a box and its name, on one line.

## Needs you

- Nothing.
