# Checks that get their secrets and publish your site, recaps read aloud, files in direct messages

## What's new

- The Chief of Staff can read its recap aloud: tap the speaker in its header. An admin picks the voice in the Workplace settings. It uses the same key as dictation.
- Direct messages carry files and pictures, and so does a message the Chief of Staff drafts for you.
- The Chief of Staff can draft a direct message to a teammate for you. It goes only when you tap Send.
- An admin who runs more than one workplace can reuse the dictation key from another of them in one tap.

## Fixed

- A secret an admin's agent grants to a repository's checks now reaches the checks that run on its protected branch. Until now the grant was accepted, and the checks never received it.
- A repository linked to a site now publishes it: once an approved change passes its checks, the site is redeployed, and its own check on the change says whether that worked. Until now nothing was published.
- Messages are ready when you open them: the messaging machine no longer goes to sleep, and a conversation opens at once from what you last saw.
- The Chief of Staff stays quick while it waits on work it handed out, even in a busy workplace.

## Needs you

- Nothing.
