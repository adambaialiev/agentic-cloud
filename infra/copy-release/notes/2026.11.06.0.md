# Upgrade from the Workspace, voice-to-text on your own key, and a tidier Workplace window

## What's new

- Upgrade from the Workspace. When a newer release is out, an admin sees "Upgrade to <version>" under Workplace › This copy. It asks once, says what will happen, and runs the upgrade on a small one-off machine in your own Fly organization. A progress page follows it, keeps going while the Workspace restarts, and puts the old release back by itself if a step fails.
- This copy now shows the newest release beside the one you run, and a "What's new" page that explains each release in plain words.
- Every release is now tried first on our own test Agentic Cloud: upgraded, rolled back on purpose, and checked, before it is offered to you.
- Voice-to-text runs on your company's own OpenRouter key. An admin saves the key under Workplace › Voice-to-text and can pick the model by its price; OpenRouter bills your account directly. Until then the microphone says it is not set up. When the account runs out of credit, or the key is refused, it says so plainly.
- Your apps directory is private to your workplace: only signed-in members can see the list of sites, and a stranger is sent to the sign-in. The sites themselves stay public at their own addresses.
- The Workplace window is one short index: people first, then rows for Messages, Work, Subscriptions and Settings, each opening its own screen, with new screens for Repositories, Sites, Storage and the Chief of Staff.
- People's cards and pages show one latest line, the week first, and their agents compactly.
- The Sites screen shows each site's preset, its estimated monthly cost on your Fly bill and any spare disks, with the total.
- The recap line at the end of a chat is easier to read, one sentence a line behind a thin rule.

## Fixed

- An agent paused for want of a sign-in can be opened and removed from its own screen.
- The daily database backup is checked before it is kept (it must open and pass an integrity check), keeps 14 daily and 8 weekly copies, and is no longer taken again every time the Workspace restarts.
- A person's All time totals name the agents no longer here without listing their hours.
- An empty Chief of Staff chat is one line that says what it is.

## Needs you

- One last upgrade by command. If you run 2026.11.01.1 or older, there is no Upgrade button yet: whoever installed your Agentic Cloud downloads this release's installer and runs its upgrade command once, in the folder they installed it from. After that, admins upgrade from Workplace › This copy.
- Optional: to turn on voice-to-text, an admin saves your company's OpenRouter key under Workplace › Voice-to-text.
