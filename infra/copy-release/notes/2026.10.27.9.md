# The first release for companies: your own Agentic Cloud, in your own cloud account

## What's new

- The whole Agentic Cloud runs in your company's own Fly organization, on your own domain: the Workspace, the code host, previews and sites, and every agent's computer.
- People join through an invite link, and an admin approves each one. Removing someone signs them out at once.
- The workplace page has a "This copy" section for admins: how each part is doing, and a diagnostics file you can download and send to support if you choose. It holds no messages, code, files, mail, keys or names.
- Upgrades come with a way back: the upgrade snapshots the database and the disks first, and puts everything back if any step fails.
- Sign-in emails can go out through any mail provider's SMTP settings.
- The Workspace's font and icons come from your own Agentic Cloud, not from our site.
